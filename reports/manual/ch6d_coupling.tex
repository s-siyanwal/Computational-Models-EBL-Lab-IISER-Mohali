\chapter{Coupled model: genetic layer to assay layer}\label{ch:coupling}

\section{Design}
The Manas genetic layer and the Chinmay assay layer stay separate programs. They are joined in one direction only:
\begin{itemize}
\item the genetic layer (\code{iasc/ibm/regime.py}) evolves populations and writes a \emph{genotype record} every generation;
\item the coupler (\code{extensions/coupler.py}, flag \code{coupling}, off by default) reads it and never writes back;
\item neither layer is refitted to the other: $\beta$ keeps its Byrne \& Rice calibration, and the record gives only each genotype's value relative to it.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Record fields}
\begin{itemize}
\item clearance $c(G)$, relative to the ancestor;
\item fecundity cost $\kappa(G,D,t)=1-\exp\!\big(-a_0\,\mathrm{tol}(G)\,D^{\nu}e^{-\delta\,c(G)\,t}\big)$, with $a_0$ set so the ancestor's 96\,h cost equals the scenario value;
\item male preference $\beta_\mathrm{rel}(G)=\max(0,1+\mathrm{BV}_b)$, where 0 means the male cannot perceive infection;
\item sex-specific 96\,h hazard $H_1D^{\nu}/c(G)$, with sex factor 1 because no independent data exist;
\item the unchanged Manas adult fitness $e^{\mathrm{BV}_f}$, $e^{\mathrm{BV}_m}$.
\end{itemize}
$\kappa=0$ and $\beta=0$ are legal genotypes, not global constants. The dose exponent $\nu=2.08$ is derived from two inputs: 50\% mortality at OD\,1.0 (Gupta 2016) and 80\% at OD\,1.5 (Chinmay).

\textbf{IUS regime in the genetic layer}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{I:} every adult is infected; survivors breed. The dose is reset each generation to keep 50\% survival, as in the protocol. A female's share is multiplied by $1-\kappa$ at egg laying (105\,h).
\item \textbf{U:} no infection, so the three traits are neutral. With zero trait effects a U generation is bit-identical to the Manas engine.
\item \textbf{Constraints:} male recombination 0; X hemizygous; ancestor burned in at $\lambda=2$ from $N\approx2500$; selection at the census of 150 + 150 ($\lambda=1$) for 60 generations (a lower bound).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Unknowns as scenarios.} The 96\,h ancestral cost (Gupta: n.s., effect size missing) and the cost's time course are \textbf{MISSING}. They are run as scenarios and never fitted: $\kappa_{96}\in\{0, 0.1, 0.3\}$, flat or decaying with a 24\,h scale.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A1 is never explained by IUS evolution.} U is unselected on $\kappa$ and $\beta$ and keeps the ancestral values. The null in U$\times$U vials therefore comes from the ancestor or from the small calibrated $\beta$.
\item \textbf{Consistent:} a modest cost (0.1--0.3) at 12--14\,h. \textbf{Excluded:} a load-driven cost near 1 at 13\,h.
\item \textbf{New derived predictions.}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item With a load-driven cost, I females should show a weaker courts-first bias than U females (+0.023 in the decay scenario; observed $-0.054$, $p=0.08$).
  \item Male genotype $\times$ infection should be null.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Prior-dependent, not fitted.} The evolved survival advantage of I is +0.22 to +0.28 (Gupta 0.35--0.40) with ancestral trait SD 0.15. It is only +0.01 to +0.02 with the raw, saturated variance.
\item \textbf{Khan:} genetic variance in $\beta$ leaves the bias (0.479 vs 0.463) and the between-vial SD (0.110) unchanged, so \textbf{H8b stays rejected}.
\item \textbf{Also unchanged:} H10, H4 and H14 keep their status. Telegony stays non-genetic. SA alleles cannot change courtship unless they change $\kappa$, $\beta$ or the cue (regression test).
\item \textbf{Male recombination audit.} Every \emph{Drosophila} run has it off. The True rows replicate the HRI preprint's own SLiM setting, in which both sexes recombine, so no HRI rerun was needed.
\end{itemize}

\section{Building it}
\begin{lstlisting}[style=sh]
python scripts/run_coupled.py                     # outputs/coupled_comparison.csv
python scripts/run_coupled.py --no-sd-scaling --tag sens_sd_realised_
NUMBA_NUM_THREADS=2 python -m pytest -q tests/test_coupling.py
\end{lstlisting}
\textbf{Regression tests}
\begin{enumerate}
\item With the genetic layer off, the assay outputs match their golden hashes. A saved Manas population (no assay traits) gives bit-identical assay output.
\item With the assay layer off, the U regime equals the Manas engine and the comparison table rebuilds unchanged.
\item An SA locus with no effect on $\kappa$, $\beta$ or the cue leaves CF, CL and CM bit-identical.
\item Male recombination is 0: paternal autosomes pass intact, and every \emph{Drosophila}-labelled result has the flag off.
\end{enumerate}
